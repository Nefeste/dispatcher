«Диспетчер 03» (Dispatcher 03) is an ambulance dispatcher's shift in a big Russian city. Someone calls and says what
happened — you ask questions, decide how urgent the call is and send a crew. Crews drive along real streets, calls pile
up, and there are always fewer free cars than you would like. The game is in Russian only.

This is a game, not training. If you need help in Russia, call 103 or 112.

## The call

The caller is rarely the patient: a daughter, a neighbour, a passer-by. Each knows something different; some get
confused, some say «I don't know». Every question costs minutes of the shift while others wait on the line. While you
talk, the shift clock stands still.

## The city

Nizhny Novgorod: streets, rivers and bridges come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Substations and
hospitals are fictional: the game says nothing about the real ambulance service.

## The review

After the shift every call is reviewed. A decision is judged by what you knew at that minute, the outcome by the truth:
bad luck is not a mistake. Signs and time limits come from the Russian Ministry of Health's orders and clinical
guidelines. There is no first-aid advice and no doses in the game, and no blood or death scenes.

## Done and in progress

«The first week» is done: seven shifts in order, a station between shifts and a free shift after the week. In progress:
a second district and a second week, flashing lights on the map, and a free shift with a
choice of district. The game will come out in RuStore for Android, offline and without sign-up.
